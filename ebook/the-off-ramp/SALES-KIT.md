# The Off-Ramp: sales kit

## Listing

**Title:** The Off-Ramp: The 90-Day Plan for Keeping Your Results After GLP-1s

**Price:** $27, or $19 as a launch price for the first week.
Why $27: it is a full system (47 pages, 12 weekly pages, 10 situation cards, 5 bonuses, printable tracker), against $5 to $7 single-sheet trackers. The prescriber worksheet and Tripwire Plan are things no competing product offers.

**Short blurb (link in bio, about 50 words):**
> You got a plan for going up on Wegovy or Zepbound. Nobody gave you one for coming off. The Off-Ramp is the 90-day workbook for the part after: a protein floor, a 20-minute strength minimum, cards for when food noise comes back, and a plan you set in advance for if the scale climbs.

**Long description (product page):**
> Roughly half of people who start a GLP-1 for weight stop within a year, often because of cost, coverage or side effects. When they stop, appetite usually returns, and most are sent off with no plan at all.
>
> The Off-Ramp is that plan. It is not a diet and it is not medical advice. It is a 12-week system that works alongside your prescriber:
> - **The Prescriber Conversation:** the 7 questions to ask before you stop, with space to write the answers.
> - **Your Floors:** your personal protein floor, strength minimum and step baseline.
> - **The Tripwire Plan:** Green, Yellow and Red zones, decided while you are calm, so a rising scale triggers a plan rather than a panic.
> - **12 weekly rules,** one page each, with a daily tick-grid.
> - **10 Food Noise Cards** for the moments that do not wait: the 9pm pantry, the "today is ruined" spiral, the overnight scale jump.
> - **The Restart Question:** a no-shame worksheet for deciding, with your doctor, whether to go back on.
> - **Bonuses:** a 7-Day Quickstart, a Protein Floor Cheat Sheet, the 20-Minute Minimum Session, word-for-word scripts, a Prescriber Visit Log and a 12-Week fridge tracker.
>
> Instant PDF download. Printable. 47 pages.

## Compliance notes for marketing

- Do not claim the book prevents weight regain, or promise a result. Say "a plan for," not "stop regain."
- Do not use brand names in ad account names or imply affiliation (Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound and Mounjaro are third-party trademarks). Descriptive use in copy is fine; logos are not.
- Paid ads on Meta and Google for weight-loss-drug topics face restricted-category review. Lead with organic content and SEO.
