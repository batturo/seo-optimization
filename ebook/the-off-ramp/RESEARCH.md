# Niche research: why "The Off-Ramp"

Date: 2026-11-18. Goal: an underserved niche with high demand, clearly defined pain points, and no real solution yet.

## Evidence for the pick

- **Discontinuation:** a 2025 study reported about 54% of patients with obesity stopped within 1 year and 72% within 2 years (as reported by News-Medical, Feb 2025). Among patients with type 2 diabetes, a Komodo claims analysis presented at ENDO 2026 put discontinuation at 41.5% at 1 year.
- **Regain:** in the STEP 1 extension, people regained about two-thirds of the weight they had lost within a year of stopping semaglutide (Wilding et al., 2022). In SURMOUNT-4, people switched to placebo regained more than half of what they had lost (Aronne et al., JAMA 2024).
- **On/off cycling:** 53.6% of people with type 2 diabetes who stopped had restarted within 1 year (Komodo, ENDO 2026). That points to a recurring, no-shame decision problem.
- **No protocol:** a 2026 clinician commentary describes "no standard-of-care transition protocol in mainstream GLP-1 prescribing" and calls the evidence base "remarkably thin."
- **Food noise returns first:** physicians quoted in 2026 coverage say the first thing patients notice after stopping is the return of cravings and appetite.
- **Competition:** Post-GLP-1 Maintenance Planner (Gumroad), GLP Reset After Plan Toolkit ($5.99), Muscle Saver journal ($6.99), plus many $2 to $14 injection and food trackers on Etsy. All are printables. None is a full system covering the prescriber conversation, food-noise moments, regain tripwires and the restart decision.

## Product positioning

A $27 system, not an information ebook. The buyer is someone who stopped recently, or is about to stop, and is afraid of regaining. The format is a 12-week workbook (one page a week), plus situation-indexed cards for food-noise moments and five bonuses. Every medication decision is routed back to the prescriber, and the book contains no dosing advice.
